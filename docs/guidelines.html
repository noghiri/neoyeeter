Community Guidelines and Standards of Conduct

Last Updated: just now

Neoyeeter ("Neoyeeter," "we," "us," or "our") operates the Neoyeeter website and related services (collectively, the "Service"). These Community Guidelines and Standards of Conduct (the "Guidelines"), together with our Privacy Policy, set out the standards that apply to everyone who accesses or uses the Service. By accessing or using the Service in any way, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agree to be bound by these Guidelines. If you do not agree, do not access or use the Service.

We may revise these Guidelines at any time. Revisions take effect immediately upon publication, as reflected in the "Last Updated" date above. Your continued use of the Service constitutes acceptance of the revised Guidelines.

We believe in modern, efficient social media experiences, equality, and the concept of innovation. We drafted these Guidelines to do three things:

  • Empowering equal treatment: No favoritism, no pet causes, absolute consistency.
  • Clarity and transparency: Our decisions are explained clearly in policy, and you will know where you stand.
  • Evolving with feedback: Because policy needs to grow to reflect the community and its needs.

Neoyeeter is moderated using a state-of-the-art in-house moderation engine, designed and operated entirely by Neoyeeter. We do not rely on AI classifiers, transformers, outsourced reviewers, volunteer moderators, or conscripts. Every account and action receives the same careful review, and is handled according to this policy. Enforcement is proportional: Identical conduct receives identical treatment. Decisions are made promptly, typically before any harm can occur, and are explained by reference to these Guidelines. If you believe we have made a mistake, you may submit an appeal. Appeals are processed as soon as possible, in the order received, via an impartial process requiring consensus among those processing the review. We reserve the right to make final decisions regarding any account or content.

Our Principles

  1. Keep people safe: We do not allow content or conduct that threatens anyone's physical safety, mental wellbeing, or privacy.
  2. Treat others with respect: We do not allow harassment, bullying, hate, or discrimination of any kind.
  3. Be genuine: We do not allow impersonation, deception, or attempts to mislead others about who you are or what you are doing.
  4. Play by the rules: We do not allow conduct that breaks the law, undermines our systems, or circumvents enforcement actions.
  5. Use the Service as intended: We do not allow automated access, scraping, or use of the Service in ways it was not designed for.

1. Keep people safe

Physical safety: We protect people from real-world harm by prohibiting threats, incitement, and the coordination of violence.

  • Do not threaten, encourage, or celebrate violence against any person or group.
  • Do not use the Service to plan, coordinate, or recruit for criminal or violent activity.
  • Do not share instructions intended to help others cause serious physical harm.

Protection of minors: We protect young people by prohibiting exploitation and by requiring that every user meet our minimum age requirements.

  • Do not create, share, or solicit content that sexualizes or exploits minors.
  • Do not contact minors for the purpose of grooming, coercion, or obtaining personal information.
  • Do not access the Service unless your age can be verified to our satisfaction and in accordance with the age-assurance requirements of your jurisdiction.

Healthy use: We support a healthy community by prohibiting patterns of use that may harm users' wellbeing.

  • Do not engage in compulsive or excessive use of the Service. What constitutes excessive use is determined at our sole discretion.
  • We may take protective action on any account where continued access could affect a user's wellbeing.

Privacy protection: We protect people's privacy by prohibiting the unauthorized collection or disclosure of personal information.

  • Do not share another person's home address, contact details, financial or medical information, or identity documents without their consent.
  • Do not disclose identifying information about any person or device, including information transmitted automatically by your software.
  • Do not submit personal data to the Service. Where personal data is received, we will act promptly to comply with applicable data protection law.
  • Neoyeeter does not collect or process identity documents, dates of birth, or biometric data.

2. Treat others with respect

Anti-harassment: We make room for everyone to participate by prohibiting harassment, bullying, and intimidation.

  • Do not repeatedly target a person with unwanted contact, insults, or abuse.
  • Do not encourage or coordinate others to harass a person, including through brigading or pile-ons.
  • Do not continue to contact a person after they have blocked you or asked you to stop.

Anti-discrimination: We protect people's dignity by prohibiting hatred and discrimination based on identity.

  • Do not attack, dehumanize, or call for the exclusion of people based on race, ethnicity, national origin, religion, caste, sex, gender identity, sexual orientation, disability, age, or serious illness.
  • Do not use slurs or hateful imagery to demean any group of people.
  • Do not promote hate groups, their symbols, or their recruitment materials.

Respectful participation: We keep conversations welcoming by prohibiting disruptive and unwelcome behavior.

  • Do not initiate contact with users who have not indicated that they wish to hear from you. Unless they have indicated otherwise, users are presumed not to wish to hear from you.
  • Do not post content that is likely to provoke disagreement.
  • Do not seek or exercise moderation authority over other users.

3. Be genuine

Account authenticity: We keep our community trustworthy by prohibiting impersonation and misrepresentation.

  • Do not impersonate any person, organization, or Neoyeeter itself.
  • Do not create or operate accounts that misrepresent who you are, who you represent, or your relationship with any other person.
  • Do not buy, sell, or transfer accounts, or offer to do so.
  • You must be able to verify your identity and the authenticity of your account upon request. Verification may be requested at any time, in any form we consider appropriate.
  • Accounts whose authenticity cannot be confirmed may be actioned. Verification is completed through our Account Recovery Center.

Transparency: We keep our moderation fair and effective by prohibiting conduct that prevents us from understanding who is using the Service.

  • Be transparent in everything you do. Our moderation systems must be able to see clearly through you at all times.
  • Do not obscure your identity, intentions, or activity from Neoyeeter's moderation systems.
  • Do not use a browser, device, or network configuration that conceals or alters information about you.
  • Transparency is assessed objectively, in accordance with ISO 13468-1:2019.
  • Accounts that cannot be adequately understood by our moderation systems may be actioned to protect the community.

Information integrity: We support reliable information sharing by prohibiting deceptive content likely to cause real-world harm.

  • Do not share false information about how, when, or where to vote, or that is intended to discourage participation in civic processes.
  • Do not share false information about active emergencies or public health threats that is likely to cause harm.
  • Do not share manipulated or synthetic media presented as authentic in a way that is likely to mislead.

4. Play by the rules

Legal compliance: We keep the Service lawful by prohibiting conduct that violates applicable law.

  • Do not use the Service to commit, facilitate, or promote any illegal activity.
  • You are responsible for complying with the laws of your jurisdiction, and of ours, and of any jurisdiction through which your data may pass.

Regulated goods and hazardous materials: We keep our community safe by prohibiting the sale, promotion, or introduction of restricted and hazardous items.

  • Do not use the Service to sell, advertise, or facilitate transactions involving weapons, controlled substances, counterfeit goods, or other regulated items.
  • To support our legal compliance, all users must be able to demonstrate compliance with California's Safe Drinking Water and Toxic Enforcement Act of 1986.

Intellectual property: We protect creators by prohibiting the unauthorized use of others' work.

  • Do not post content that infringes another party's copyright, trademark, or other intellectual property rights.
  • Do not remove attribution, watermarks, or other ownership information from content you share.

Site security: We keep the Service running smoothly by prohibiting interference with our systems.

  • Do not attempt to access any part of the Service you are not authorized to access. Authorization is extended only to accounts in good standing.
  • Do not attempt to probe, scan, disrupt, or circumvent our systems, security features, or enforcement measures.

Repeated violations and ban evasion: We keep enforcement fair by prohibiting attempts to avoid it.

  • Do not create a new account, or use any other means, to access the Service after an enforcement action, or in anticipation of one.
  • Accessing the Service without an account in good standing is considered ban evasion.

The spirit of these Guidelines: These Guidelines cannot anticipate every situation.

  • Conduct that violates the principles described in these Guidelines may result in enforcement action even where no specific rule applies.

5. Use the Service as intended

Automated access: We keep the Service reliable by prohibiting unauthorized automation.

  • Do not access the Service using bots, scrapers, crawlers, or any other user agent as defined in RFC 9110, Section 3.5, without our prior written consent.
  • For the purposes of these Guidelines, automated access includes the use of any tool that sends requests to the Service without direct human input for each request.

Spam and platform manipulation: We keep conversations genuine by prohibiting spam and the artificial manipulation of engagement.

  • Do not post repetitive, unsolicited, or bulk content, including commercial spam and engagement bait.
  • Do not artificially inflate likes, follows, reposts, or other engagement metrics, or buy or sell engagement.
  • Do not coordinate networks of accounts to amplify content or mislead others about its popularity.

Intended use: We keep the Service focused on its purpose by prohibiting uses it was not designed to support.

  • The Service is designed to provide a private, safe, and perfectly consistently moderated experience. Do not use it in any way that is inconsistent with that design.
  • Features not available to your account may not be used.

6. Protected expression

These Guidelines are designed to protect legitimate expression that serves the public interest. The following content is welcome on Neoyeeter:

  • Journalism, research, and educational content on matters of public interest.
  • Advocacy, commentary, and criticism, including criticism of Neoyeeter.
  • Satire and parody, where clearly identifiable as such.
  • Personal experiences shared to support others.

Protected expression remains subject to all other provisions of these Guidelines.
