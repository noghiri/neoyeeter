Our Mission

We believe in modern, efficient social media experiences, equality, and the concept of innovation. We want to unlock a new era of safe, effortless posting for all, without forgetting that every user is an individual with their own wants, hopes, and dreams. To accomplish this, everything we build follows a few core principles:

Energy efficiency without compromise

We've optimized the user experience down to the essentials. No gigawatts or hyperscale buildout needed.

Absolute security

We are the only social media company that can truthfully promise that your account on our service will never be compromised. Ever.

Human-safe design

You'll never get a single notification from us behind the wheel. Or anywhere else.

Perfectionism is a valid target

We aren't willing to settle for AI classifiers or underpaid offshore contractors or volunteer labor. Perfectly professional moderation, guaranteed.

Conceptual innovation

We believe that there exist multiple concepts, and one of them is innovation.
